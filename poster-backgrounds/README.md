# Approved poster backgrounds

Upload original PNG/JPG/JPEG images with unique simple filenames (letters, digits, hyphens and underscores). Existing backgrounds must keep their stable filenames because posters store the background ID, not a copy of its image. The optimization workflow produces full-resolution WebP images (up to 2480 × 3508), small WebP previews (up to 240 × 340) and the catalog. Original sources are retained. Publish the generated assets and catalog together with the site; verify the Pages deployment after any future asset update.

The editor accepts only catalog entries and defaults to the first approved image. Previously saved custom backgrounds are not rendered by the gallery editor; underlying old records and files are not deleted. Product and logo image uploads are unchanged. The chosen image is an actual poster image layer, used in both preview and print. The cloud snapshot stores only `design.bgId`, not a background upload.
